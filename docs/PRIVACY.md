# RT Trainer privacy policy

_Last updated: 18 October 2026_

RT Trainer doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## Microphone and speech recognition

The app uses your microphone only while you hold the transmit key. Your speech is converted to text by Apple's Speech framework so the app can check your phraseology:

- With **Aviation-tuned recognition on this Mac** turned on (the default), recognition happens on your Mac.
- With it turned off, audio is sent to Apple for recognition under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). RT Trainer's developer never receives it.

Transcripts and audio aren't saved.

## Data stored on your Mac

Your settings (registration, aircraft type, voice preferences) and your best score for each scenario are kept on your Mac only. Deleting the app removes them.

## Network access

When you choose **Check for New Scenarios**, the app downloads scenario files from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). No information about you is sent, apart from what any web request reveals, such as your IP address, which GitHub handles under its own [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
